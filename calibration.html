Calibration

Compare the tester’s force readings with known forces, and record the result.

Tare is not a calibration. Tare only sets the current reading to zero. A calibration compares the readings with known forces. This page does not change any reading on the tester or on this site; it only helps you work out and record a correction.

Enter points

For each known mass, type the force the tester reported (in newtons) once the reading has settled. The force from the mass is calculated as F = m·g, where m is a mass (grams or kilograms), not a force.

What this page does and does not do

  • It fits a straight line, reading = gain × force + offset, to the points you type. It does not measure anything.
  • The correction it suggests is not applied anywhere: not on this site, not on the tester. Any correction would have to go into the controller code, which does not exist yet.
  • It makes no claim of traceability or of meeting a standard. A calibration is only as good as the masses used; their accuracy is not known to this page.
  • The standard value of g (9.80665 m/s²) is a convention. The local value differs slightly.
  • Nothing you enter is saved or uploaded. It stays in this browser tab until you download the record.
