Stress Relaxation – New test

Specimen extended to a set displacement and held while force is recorded. Output: stress change σ(t) during the hold and the apparent stress/strain ratio after the ramp. Read the protocol.

Safety: the on-screen STOP button is not an emergency stop. Keep the machine’s physical emergency-stop button within reach; it must work even if this page, the computer, or the cable fails.

Governing equations
QuantityEquationDefinition
Applied strainε0=ΔLL0Strain imposed during loading and then held constant for t > 0.
Stressσ(t)=F(t)A0Engineering stress from the force recorded during the hold (MPa).
Relaxation functionG(t)=σ(t)σ0Stress remaining relative to the initial stress σ0 = σ(0). Dimensionless, 1 at t = 0 (not the shear modulus).
Percent relaxed100[1−σ(t)σ0]Fraction of the initial stress lost by time t.
Apparent stress/strain ratio after rampE(t)=σ(t)ε0Time-dependent modulus at constant strain (MPa or GPa).
Prony seriesσ(t)=σ∞+∑iσie−tτiσ∞: equilibrium stress. σi, τi: amplitude and time constant of relaxation mode i.
Endpoint-referenced 1/e decay timeσ(τ)=σend+σ0−σendeTime for the decaying part of the stress to fall to 1/e (about 37%). Reported here as a single characteristic value, with σend the stress at the end of the hold.

Strain is held constant, so stress decay reflects time-dependent (viscoelastic) behavior. Reference method: ASTM E328 for stress relaxation of materials; protocols for biological tissue vary.
