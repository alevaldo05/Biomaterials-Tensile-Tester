Choose a test

Select a test to set up the specimen and run it on the tester.

  • Uniaxial Tension

    Extend a specimen at a commanded crosshead speed while recording force and displacement. Review the engineering stress–strain curve and method-dependent estimates of modulus, strength, and endpoint strain.

  • Stress Relaxation

    Ramp to a target displacement, then hold while recording force. Review stress change during the hold. Crosshead position does not establish constant specimen strain.

Not sure how to run it? Read the Uniaxial Tension or Stress Relaxation protocol. To open an earlier test, use Load Test.
