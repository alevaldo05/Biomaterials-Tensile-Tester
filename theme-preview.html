Theme preview

Draft Not part of the site. For choosing colors only; nothing here changes a theme.

Seven color sets, each showing the same samples: a header, buttons, a link, a card, a table, a warning, a small graph, and a keyboard focus ring. The numbers under each set are measured in this browser from the set's own colors (text needs 4.5 to 1; the mark, focus ring, and control border need 3 to 1).

Manhattan University colors: the values for the three MU sets come from the project plan and are unverified; they have not been compared with the official guidelines. Colors only: no logo, seal, or wordmark is used, and the set is named “MU colors”, not an official theme.

What to decide

  1. Keep today’s green, or switch the green themes to MU green (the “MU green” sets).
  2. Add the separate “MU colors” theme, or not.
  3. Accept the two dark themes (Purple dark, Pink dark), or ask for changes.
