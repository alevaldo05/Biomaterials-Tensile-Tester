CSV format

Files downloaded from a New Test page open directly, with their dimensions and test details. Other files work too: the first row is a header, and columns are matched by keyword (any case). Put units in the name, for example force_N, displacement_mm, stress_MPa, time_s, strain_%.

Uniaxial tension: displacement plus force, or strain plus stress.

Stress relaxation: time plus force or stress (optionally a displacement or strain column for the hold). Time is shifted to start at 0.

Comma, semicolon, or tab separated; at least 10 rows; up to 20 MB. Lines starting with # are notes. Without saved dimensions, force and displacement are converted with example dimensions that you can edit below the graph.
